Transparent Minting

Mint authority retained under a 3-of-5 multisig. New mints only when the charity allocation is fully used, capped in +250B tranches. Global ceiling: 2T total supply without ≥80% holder approval.

Donations Flow

Milestones unlock tranches. Swap proceeds: 90% → charity, 10% → dev/ops for sustainability. All donations published with receipts + on-chain tx. Community votes choose causes.

Launch & Presale

Presale hard cap example: 500 SOL at a fair discount; 80% to Raydium LP (locked), remainder to dev/ops + first donation. Tokens distributed post-listing.

Is supply fixed?

Initial 1T. Future mints only when the charity wallet is depleted, capped at +250B/cycle, never above 2T without 80% approval.

How do donations happen?

Milestones trigger planned sell-offs; 90% of proceeds to the chosen charity, 10% to dev/ops. We publish tx hashes + receipts.

What keeps this ethical?

LP locked, multisig control, public wallets, on-chain votes, time-locks, and a clear, pre-declared policy for inflation.

Where will it trade?

We mint SPL before presale. No DEX listing until liquidity is added on Raydium. Official pool address announced at launch.
