08 — Mechanics, as drawn above

What this mockup assumes

Trigger
Rejections and voids only. Never an approval — an approval already pays.
Odds
1 in 2,000 rejections. A parameter, not a design choice yet.
Prize
5% of Treasury at the moment it fires, locked to tokens at that price like any award.
Cycle limit
Does not consume the one-approval-per-cycle slot: it is not an approval.
Claim
Same claim code, same 7-day expiry, same multisig. Nothing new to build there.
Handle
Optional, validated ^[A-Za-z0-9_]{4,15}$, stored per proposal.

Cost of the idea. At 1 in 2,000 and a £20k treasury, one golden ticket costs £1,000 — and the fee income from 2,000 submissions at $0.40 is roughly $800, of which 35% reaches the Treasury. As drawn, the ticket drains the Treasury faster than fees refill it. The odds and the 5% both need to be solved against real numbers, not chosen because they sound good.

Two things the code would need first. Sweeping is still unbuilt, so no fee money reaches the Treasury at all yet. And the publisher deliberately strips @mentions from every ruling before posting — tagging a handle needs a deliberate hole in that guard, not an accident.
